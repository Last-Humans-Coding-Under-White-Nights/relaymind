Role: second, independent relevance rater. You work alone and see nothing
but the requests and the asset list at the end of this message.

Method, for every request:
- Read the request and decide what the user is trying to get done.
- Scan the whole asset list (lines "Annnn [kind] name — description"). Keep
  the assets whose described function would let an assistant do that job.
  Consider assets written in Chinese and in English equally.
- Decide the label:
  positive  = at least one kept asset fits the job;
  negative  = nothing in the list fits;
  ambiguous = the request could mean clearly different jobs that different
              assets serve, and it does not say which (having two equivalent
              assets for one job is still positive).
- List the kept handles under "acceptable" (none for negative, at most 8).
- language_valid: is the text fluent and in the language the lane declares
  (code_switch = Chinese mixed with English)?
- independent_case: does it read as a real, self-contained request?
- notes: one sentence.

Output format (JSON only, no prose):
{"judgments":[{"case_id":"...","class":"positive|negative|ambiguous","acceptable":["A123"],"language_valid":true,"independent_case":true,"notes":"..."}]}
